About

The name comes from something simple: Reeds don't survive a storm by standing rigid — they bend, and that's exactly what lets them stay whole. That's the whole idea behind this space. This is where I write about yoga and movement. This isn't a place for perfect poses or polished routines — it's notes from someone still figuring it out, one session, one fall, one page at a time.
