Starting without knowing what this is yet

I've been building this platform for a few days now. I don't have a plan for it — I just didn't want to keep putting it off.

That's actually the same thing I always told people when they came to their first yoga session with me. They usually apologize for not being flexible, or not knowing the poses, like there's a version of themselves they should already be before they start. I tell them the same thing I'm telling myself right now: you don't need to know where this goes. You just start, and you adjust as you go.

So this is me adjusting as I go. First entry, no plan, more soon.
